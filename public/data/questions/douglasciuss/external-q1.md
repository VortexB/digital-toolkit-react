# External political or economic changes impacting on the implementing organisation could threaten the introduction of the innovation.

## Recommended Actions
1. Assess if the new technology and the proposed changes to services align with the strategic priorities for the wider health system (e.g., health policy, national priorities for actions and improvement).
   * [E-mental health strategy for Canada](https://mentalhealthcommission.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/An-E-Mental-Health-Strategy-for-Canada-FINAL.pdf) (Mental Health Commission of Canada).
   * [Douglas Research Centre strategic plan (2021-2025)](https://douglas.research.mcgill.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/202104_strategicplan_0.pdf)
   * [Santé Québec strategic plan (2025-2028)](https://sante.quebec/documents-et-autres-ressources/)
2. Try to align the project with current policy priorities by trying to 'rebrand' your work to fit these.
3. Keep a close eye on the outer context.
