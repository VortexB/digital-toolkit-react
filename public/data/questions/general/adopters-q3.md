# Some or all individuals or teams have limited resources, time, space or support to learn to use the innovation.

## Recommended Actions
1. For service users, identifying times within patient treatment schedules when they are available to engage with the technology and provide technical support. [(Park, S. Y., Sigmon, C. N., &amp; Boeldt, D. (2022). A framework for the implementation of digital mental health interventions)](https://assets.cureus.com/uploads/review_article/pdf/109121/20221019-18782-1sguwj6.pdf)
2. For clinicians, embed learning in the workflow (suitable tools and guides to encourage innovation) for easy access and demonstrations of content (e.g., video creation, use of a dashboard). [(Balcombe, L., &amp; De Leo, D. (2021). Digital mental health challenges and the horizon ahead for solutions. JMIR Mental Health, 8(3),e26811)](https://mental.jmir.org/2021/3/e26811)
3. Have technology specialists or digital navigators tasked with introducing innovations to service users. [(Connolly, S.L., Hogan, T.P., Shimada, S.L. et al.(2021). Leveraging Implementation Science to Understand Factors Influencing Sustained Use of Mental Health Apps: a Narrative Review. J. technol. behav. sci.6, 184–196)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41347-020-00165-4).
4. Identify and implement effective strategies through [StrategEase Tool](https://thecenterforimplementation.com/strategease-tool) StrategEase Tool(The Center for Implementation. (2022). StrategEase Tool).
