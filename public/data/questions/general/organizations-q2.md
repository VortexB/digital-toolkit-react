# The implementing organization has limited resources (people or money) to channel into innovative projects.

## Recommended Actions
1. Budget adequate for staff to learn and adjust as the transition occurs.
2. Test commercial feasibility and technological development by financing and supporting projects in the start-up phase (INSSS Bulletin v.10).
3. Consider a recurrent budget line (e.g., licenses, IT support).
4. Have a digital navigator (a dedicated health care profession whose task in hospitals and clinics would be to select evidence-based apps, troubleshoot, and interpret digital data outputs in a clinically meaningful way) to introduce and support the use of a novel technology. (Spadaro, B., Martin-Key, N. A., & Bahn, S. (2021). Building the digital mental health ecosystem: opportunities and challenges for mobile health innovators. Journal of Medical Internet Research, 23(10), e27507.
