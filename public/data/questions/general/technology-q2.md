# There are significant privacy concerns over your digital health innovations.

## Recommended Actions
1. Consult the [Digital Privacy Playbook](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-privacy-playbook.html) (Government of Canada) and [Privacy and Security Guideline](https://www.infoway-inforoute.ca/en/component/edocman/6475-digital-health-solutions-privacy-security-guideline/view-document?Itemid=103) (Canada Health Infoway) to start planning for privacy considerations
2. Check the relevant privacy regulations (Federal/Provincial):
   * [The Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) - Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/)
   * [Datum project - List of regulations](https://www.mcgill.ca/datum/library/legislation)
3. Check the privacy policy of the technology developed by the third party to comply with the institutional privacy and data security guideline.
4. Consult the IT department for data security protocol and requirement.
