# The adoption and implementation of the innovation may create additional work for clinicians.

## Recommended Actions
1. Involve managers from the early stage of adoption so they can informthe need for changes and how clinicians and/or patients may benefit fromthem.
2. Clearly communicate the changes to allow for preparation.(Nilsen, P., Seing, I., Ericsson, C. et al. Characteristics of successfulchanges in health care organizations: an interview study with physicians,registered nurses and assistant nurses. BMC Health Serv Res 20, 147(2020). https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-020-4999-8).
3. Listen to concerns.
4. Assist with onboarding (e.g., educational materials, point-person).
5. Design and offer training.
6. Engage local champions to address (potential) resistance to change.
7. Identify effective strategies through StrategEase Tool.(The Center for Implementation. (2022). StrategEase Tool).
