# There are concerns that the technology could widen inequalities while improving care/access for some patients.

## Recommended Actions
1. Aim to ensure equity regarding who can access the innovation.
2. Identify unintended potential health impacts on vulnerable or marginalized populations.
   * [Health Equity Impact Assessment (HEIA) | CAMH](https://www.camh.ca/en/professionals/professionals--projects/heia)
   * [Assessment Framework for Mental Health Apps](https://mentalhealthcommission.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/MHCC-Assessment-Framework-for-Mental-Health-Apps-EN-FINAL.pdf) (Mental Health Commission of Canada): Consult "Cultural Safety, Social Responsibility & Equity".
3. Try inclusive practices when co-designing with socially excluded persons ([Guide](https://ssaquebec.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Loignon-et-al.-2022-Inclusive-practices-in-PPR-with-socially-excluded-persons.pdf)).
4. Consult the Accessibility Standards and Technical Guide to remove potential accessibility barriers and improve accessibility (Government of Canada).
   * Technical guide: [Accessible and Equitable Artificial Intelligence Systems](https://accessible.canada.ca/sites/default/files/2024-09/technicalguide-artificialintelligence-asc.pdf)
   * Standards: [Accessibility requirements for ICT products and services](https://accessible.canada.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/can-asc-en301549-20240226-v02-en-aoda.pdf)
5. Tailor and offer [Digital Outreach for Obtaining Resources and Skills (DOORS)](https://www.digitalpsych.org/doors-program.html) program to support development of digital literacy skills of service users (particularly those with severe mental illness).
