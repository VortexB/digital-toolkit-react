# Domain 2: There are concerns that the technology could widen inequalities while improving care/access for some patients.

## Recommended Actions
1. Aim to ensure equity regarding who can access the innovation.
2. Identify unintended potential health impacts on vulnerable ormarginalized populations. Health Equity Impact Assessment (HEIA) | CAMH Assessment Framework for Mental Health Apps (MentalHealth Commission of Canada): Consult “Cultural Safety,Social Responsibility &amp; Equity”.
3. Try inclusive practices when co-designing with socially excludedpersons (Guide).
4. Consult the Accessibility Standards and Technical Guide toremove potential accessibility barriers and improve accessibility(Government of Canada). Technical guide: Accessible and Equitable ArtificialIntelligence Systems15 Standards: Accessibility requirements for ICT products andservices
5. Tailor and offer Digital Outreach for Obtaining Resources andSkills (DOORS) program to support development of digitalliteracy skills of service users (particularly those with severemental illness).Domain 3: Intended adopters of innovation/technologyThis domain looks at the complexity of the adopter system, i.e., clinicians, staff, patientsand carers who are expected to use the technology but who may refuse to use it or findthat they are unable to use it.
