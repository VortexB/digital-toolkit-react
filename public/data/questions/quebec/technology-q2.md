# Domain 1: There are significant privacy concerns over your digital health innovations.

## Recommended Actions
1. Please be aware that, in Quebec, the [Privacy Impact Assessment/Évaluation des facteurs relatifs à la vie privée (EFVP)](https://cdn.ciusss-ouestmtl.gouv.qc.ca/Recherche__enseignement_et_innovation/3._Presentation_aux_chercheurs_-_EFVP_PRP_-_novembre_2024_-_Version_PDF_partageable.pdf) is required if your research needs to access medical information or charts without consent.

   Also see:
   * [Law 5: Act respecting health and social services information](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/R-22.1)
   * [Law 25: Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/p-39.1)

2. Check the privacy policy of the technology developed by the third party to comply with the institutional privacy and data security guidelines.

   **For CIUSSS de L'Ouest-de-l'île-de-Montréal/Douglas respondents:**

3. Contact the website of the CIUSSS de L'Ouest-de-l'île-de-Montréal research support team ([L'Unité de Soutien et d'Encadrement de la Recherche (USER)](https://www.ciusss-ouestmtl.gouv.qc.ca/recherche-enseignement-et-innovation/recherche) for the updated information and procedures.

4. Consult the DRIGBM (Information Resources and Biomedical Management Directorate) for the data security protocol and requirements (e.g., a security questionnaire, compliance with ISO 27001/02, ISO 27017, la loi québécoise sur la protection de la vie privée 25, and Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act).
   
5. If the third-party technology does not have a recent penetration test report, ISO, and other compliance, contact the CIUSSS IT for penetration test done by the Quebec Ministry of Health and Social Services.
